    Welcome to Microverse

    1. Create & edit: Pause, choose a terrain brush and its size and shape, then click or drag on the world to paint. Use Erase to clear back to grass.
    2. Introduce animals: Select + Rabbit or + Wolf and click a valid land tile. Animals behave autonomously.
    3. Run the simulation: Press Play, adjust speed (1x/2x/4x), and watch time and events unfold.
    4. Inspect: Use Select to click a tile or creature for details on energy, hunger, age, behavior, and hunts.
    5. Understand change: Track populations, births/deaths, hunts, grass, history graph, and the event log.
